# Common Questions

---

### Which frameworks does Vissibl support?

Vissibl is framework-agnostic. We can support any compliance framework, including ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, SOC 2, API Q2, PDPL, and many more. You can also upload internal standards or client requirements. If you're unsure whether your specific framework is supported, ask your account manager.

### Can I manage multiple frameworks together?

Yes. You can run multiple frameworks within a single workspace as an Integrated Management System (IMS). Vissibl maps shared clauses across frameworks so you're not duplicating work.

### How many users can I add?

Unlimited users are included with every subscription. There are no per-seat or per-headcount charges.

### Does Vissibl support Arabic?

Yes. The platform includes an English and Arabic toggle so users can work in their preferred language.

### What happens when an ISO standard is revised?

When a framework update is officially published, Vissibl ingests the changes and drafts the necessary updates to your documentation. These appear as draft changes for your review. Nothing is published automatically. You'll see what's changed and why, and go through your normal review and approval process before anything goes live.

### Can Vissibl conduct an internal audit on our behalf?

Yes. This is an optional service. If you'd prefer not to conduct your own internal audit, our team can audit your system for you. Contact your account manager to arrange it.

### Can I import data from my existing systems?

During onboarding, our team will import the documents and data you provide, including risk registers, training records, complaints logs, and more. If you'd prefer a clean start rather than importing historical data, that's fine too.

### What do I need to provide during onboarding?

To get the most out of onboarding, try to gather the following ahead of your onboarding call:

* Existing QMS/IMS documentation (policies, procedures, work instructions)
* Organisational chart (even a draft or high-level version)
* Risk register
* Audit program and any recent audit findings
* Quality objectives / KPIs
* Vendor or approved supplier list
* Non-conformity and corrective action records
* Training records
* Customer feedback or complaints register
* Any forms or checklists used in your operations

Don't worry if some of these are incomplete or missing. That's what the onboarding process is designed to address.

### How long does onboarding take?

It depends on the complexity of your system and how quickly you can provide documentation. Most customers are up and running within one to two weeks. If you have a specific deadline (e.g. an upcoming surveillance audit), let us know and we'll plan around it.

### Is my data secure?

Vissibl is cloud-native and built on GCP and Azure. We adhere to enterprise-grade security protocols including encryption at rest and in transit. Your documents are only accessible to users within your workspace. You can also enable two-factor authentication from Settings > Security.

### Can data be hosted locally?

Depending on your region and requirements, we have the ability to host data locally. Contact your account manager to discuss options.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Individual documents and reports can be downloaded from within the platform at any time. You can also delete your workspace and data from Settings > Company Profile.

### What support is available after onboarding?

You have access to in-app support directly within Vissibl. For compliance-related questions, you also have ongoing access to a dedicated ISO subject matter expert. See [Contacting Support](contacting-support.md) for all the ways to reach our team.
