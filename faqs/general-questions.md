# Common Questions

## What is Vissibl?

Vissibl is a compliance operations platform with an AI co-pilot. It brings your quality, health, safety, and environmental (QHSE) management and vendor oversight into one place.

## Which ISO standards does Vissibl support?

Vissibl supports ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 14001 (Environmental), ISO 45001 (Health & Safety), and Integrated Management Systems (IMS).

## How does the AI work?

Vissibl's AI assistant, Vissi, uses your uploaded documents and company context to answer compliance questions. It provides citations so you can verify every answer against the source.

## Can I import my existing documents?

Yes. Upload your existing files (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, PPTX) to the Vault. Vissibl will extract metadata, index the content, and use it for gap analysis and AI-powered answers.

## How many users can I add?

This depends on your subscription plan. Check **Settings → Billing** for your current plan details, or contact your account manager.

## Is my data secure?

Yes. Vissibl uses enterprise-grade security including encryption at rest and in transit. Your documents are only accessible to users within your workspace.
