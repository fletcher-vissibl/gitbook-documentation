# ISO 14001 Essentials

### What is ISO 14001?

ISO 14001 is the international standard for Environmental Management Systems (EMS). It helps your organisation identify and manage its environmental impact, comply with environmental legislation, and demonstrate environmental responsibility.

When you add ISO 14001 to your Vissibl workspace, the platform maps your documentation and evidence to the standard's environmental requirements.

### What an EMS requires

An environmental management system under ISO 14001 means you need to:

* **Identify your environmental aspects.** These are the ways your activities interact with the environment: emissions, waste, water use, energy consumption, chemical storage, and so on.
* **Assess significance.** Not all aspects carry the same weight. You need to determine which ones are significant and need active management.
* **Set environmental objectives.** Targets for reducing or managing your impact.
* **Implement operational controls.** Procedures and practices that manage your significant aspects.
* **Monitor and measure.** Track your environmental performance over time.

In Vissibl, environmental risks live in your Risk Assessment register, environmental procedures sit in Documentation under ISO 14001, and evidence of monitoring goes into the Evidence section.

### How it differs from ISO 9001

If you're already running ISO 9001, the good news is the structure is almost identical (both follow Annex SL). The key differences:

* **Focus.** ISO 9001 is about customer satisfaction and product quality. ISO 14001 is about environmental impact and legal compliance.
* **Unique requirement.** ISO 14001 requires an environmental aspect and impact assessment. ISO 9001 doesn't have an equivalent.
* **Interested parties.** ISO 9001 focuses on customers. ISO 14001 brings in regulators, local communities, and environmental groups.
* **Legal emphasis.** Environmental legislation plays a bigger role. You need to identify and track your compliance obligations.

Because both standards share the same structure, Vissibl can map shared clauses across frameworks when you run them as an IMS. See the Integrated Management System page for more.
