# ISO 14001 Essentials

## What is ISO 14001?

ISO 14001 is the international standard for **Environmental Management Systems (EMS)**. It helps organisations minimise their environmental impact, comply with environmental legislation, and demonstrate environmental responsibility.

## Environmental management basics

An EMS under ISO 14001 requires you to:

* Identify your **environmental aspects** — the ways your activities interact with the environment (e.g., emissions, waste, water use).
* Assess the **significance** of each aspect.
* Set **environmental objectives** and targets for improvement.
* Implement **operational controls** to manage significant aspects.
* Monitor and measure your environmental performance.

## Key differences from ISO 9001

| Area | ISO 9001 | ISO 14001 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Customer satisfaction and product/service quality | Environmental impact and legal compliance |
| **Key requirement** | Meeting customer requirements | Identifying and managing environmental aspects |
| **Unique element** | Customer feedback and satisfaction monitoring | Environmental aspect/impact assessment, compliance obligations |
| **Interested parties** | Primarily customers | Regulators, communities, environmental groups |

Both standards share the same high-level structure (Annex SL), making them easy to integrate.
