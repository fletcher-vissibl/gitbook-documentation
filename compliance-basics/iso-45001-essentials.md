# ISO 45001 Essentials

## What is ISO 45001?

ISO 45001 is the international standard for **Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) Management Systems**. It provides a framework for preventing work-related injury and ill health, and for providing safe and healthy workplaces.

## Occupational health & safety basics

An OH&S management system under ISO 45001 requires you to:

* Identify **hazards** and assess **risks** to workers.
* Determine applicable **legal and other requirements**.
* Set **OH&S objectives** and plan how to achieve them.
* Establish **operational controls** for identified risks.
* Prepare for and respond to **emergency situations**.
* Monitor and measure OH&S performance.

## Key differences from ISO 9001

| Area | ISO 9001 | ISO 45001 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Product/service quality | Worker health and safety |
| **Key requirement** | Customer requirements | Hazard identification and risk assessment |
| **Unique element** | Customer satisfaction | Worker consultation and participation |
| **Legal focus** | Product/service regulations | Health and safety legislation |

ISO 45001 places special emphasis on **worker participation** — consulting workers on OH&S matters and giving them a voice in the management system.
