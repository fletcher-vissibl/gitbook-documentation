# ISO 45001 Essentials

### What is ISO 45001?

ISO 45001 is the international standard for Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) Management Systems. It gives you a framework for preventing work-related injury and illness, and for providing safe and healthy workplaces.

When you add ISO 45001 to your Vissibl workspace, the platform maps your health and safety documentation, risk assessments, and evidence to the standard's requirements.

### What an OH&S management system requires

Under ISO 45001, you need to:

* **Identify hazards and assess risks to workers.** Not just physical hazards, but also psychosocial risks, ergonomic issues, and organisational factors.
* **Determine legal requirements.** Know which health and safety laws and regulations apply to your operations.
* **Set OH&S objectives.** Targets for improving safety performance.
* **Establish operational controls.** Procedures for managing identified risks.
* **Prepare for emergencies.** Have documented emergency response procedures and make sure your team knows them.
* **Monitor and measure.** Track incidents, near misses, and safety performance indicators.

In Vissibl, safety risks live in the Risk Assessment register, safety procedures sit in Documentation under ISO 45001, and incident records can be tracked through Non Conformities and Registers.

### How it differs from ISO 9001

* **Focus.** ISO 9001 is about product and service quality. ISO 45001 is about keeping people safe.
* **Unique requirement.** ISO 45001 places special emphasis on worker consultation and participation. Workers need a voice in the management system, not just management.
* **Legal focus.** Health and safety legislation is central. Regulators can shut you down if you're not compliant.
* **Hazard identification.** ISO 45001 requires a systematic approach to identifying hazards, which goes beyond the risk-based thinking in ISO 9001.

Like ISO 14001, ISO 45001 follows the same Annex SL structure as ISO 9001, making it straightforward to integrate in Vissibl.
