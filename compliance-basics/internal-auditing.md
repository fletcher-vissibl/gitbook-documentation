# Internal Auditing

### Why internal audits matter

Internal audits are a requirement of ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001. They're your chance to find and fix problems before an external auditor does. A good internal audit programme is one of the strongest signs of a mature management system.

Think of internal audits as a health check for your system, not a test you pass or fail.

### How audits work in Vissibl

Go to **My Audits** in the sidebar to see all your audits in one table. Each audit shows its ID, name, type, framework, dates, department, and auditor. You can run internal audits, external audits, and supplier audits.

You can also trigger a **Vissi Audit** from the Frameworks page. This is an AI-powered audit that runs automatically against your documentation and evidence.

### Creating an audit

1. Go to **My Audits** and click **+ Audit**.
2. Fill in the audit details: name, description, audit type (Internal, External, or Supplier Audit), compliance framework, start and end dates, department being audited, auditee name, and internal or external auditor name.
3. Select the **clauses** you want to audit against. You can select as many or as few as needed for the scope of the audit.

### Working through the clauses

Click the **Clauses** tab on any audit. You'll see every selected clause listed with its requirement description. For each requirement, you have two options:

**Conform.** Click the green Conform button if the requirement is being met. You can add a description explaining how it's met and upload supporting documents as evidence.

**Raise NCR.** Click the red Raise NCR button if the requirement is not being met. This opens a form where you fill in the type, observation, source, date reported, priority, and evidence. Clicking Submit NCR creates the non-conformity directly in the Non Conformities board, linked to this specific audit.

This means your audit findings flow straight into your NCR tracking without any manual re-entry.

### Impartiality

The ISO standards require that auditors do not audit their own work. This means:

* The quality manager shouldn't audit the quality department.
* Arrange cross-departmental audits where possible.
* If your team is small, consider using Vissibl's optional internal audit service. Our team can audit your system for you. Contact your account manager to arrange it.

### Tips

* Spread your internal audits across the year rather than doing everything at once. This gives you continuous coverage and avoids the pre-audit scramble.
* Use the audit to talk to the people actually doing the work. You'll learn more from a conversation with an operator than from reviewing a document.
* Finding non-conformities during an internal audit is a good thing. It shows your system is working.
* NCRs raised during an audit are automatically linked to that audit. You can filter the Non Conformities board by audit to see all findings from a specific audit cycle.
