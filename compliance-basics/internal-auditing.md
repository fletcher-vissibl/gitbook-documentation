# Internal Auditing

## Why internal audits matter

Internal audits are a **requirement** of ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001. They're your opportunity to find and fix problems before an external auditor does. A good internal audit programme is one of the strongest indicators of a mature management system.

## Department-based vs. procedure-based auditing

* **Department-based** — Audit each department against the clauses relevant to their work. This is Vissibl's default approach and is easier to schedule and manage.
* **Procedure-based** — Audit specific procedures or processes across the organisation. Useful for cross-functional processes.

## Impartiality requirements

The ISO standards require that auditors **do not audit their own work**. This means:

* The quality manager shouldn't audit the quality department.
* Arrange cross-departmental audits where possible.
* If your team is small, consider using external auditors for some internal audits.

## Recording findings

For each audit finding, record:

* **What was observed** — The factual evidence.
* **Which clause it relates to** — Link to the specific framework requirement.
* **Whether it conforms or not** — Conform, non-conform, or observation.
* **Corrective actions required** — If non-conforming, what needs to change.

Vissibl's audit checklists handle this structure automatically.
