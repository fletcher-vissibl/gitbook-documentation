# Running an Integrated Management System (IMS)

## What is an IMS?

An Integrated Management System combines two or more management system standards (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001) into a single, unified system. Instead of running separate systems with duplicated processes, you manage everything together.

## Benefits of integrating 9001 / 14001 / 45001

* **Reduced duplication** — Shared processes like document control, internal audits, and management reviews are managed once.
* **Simpler auditing** — Combined audits cover multiple standards in a single visit.
* **Better resource efficiency** — Less paperwork, fewer meetings, lower costs.
* **Holistic risk management** — Quality, environmental, and safety risks are considered together.
* **Consistent culture** — One management system means one way of working.
