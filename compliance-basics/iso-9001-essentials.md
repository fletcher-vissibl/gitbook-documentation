# ISO 9001 Essentials

## What is ISO 9001?

ISO 9001 is the international standard for **Quality Management Systems (QMS)**. It provides a framework for consistently delivering products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.

It's the most widely adopted ISO management system standard in the world, used by organisations of all sizes and industries.

## Key clauses explained in plain English

ISO 9001 is structured around 10 clauses. The key ones you'll work with are:

* **Clause 4 — Context of the Organisation** — Understand your business environment, stakeholders, and the scope of your QMS.
* **Clause 5 — Leadership** — Top management must demonstrate commitment and establish a quality policy.
* **Clause 6 — Planning** — Identify risks and opportunities, set quality objectives, and plan how to achieve them.
* **Clause 7 — Support** — Ensure you have the right resources, competencies, and documented information.
* **Clause 8 — Operation** — Plan and control your operational processes, from design to delivery.
* **Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation** — Monitor, measure, and evaluate your QMS performance through audits and management reviews.
* **Clause 10 — Improvement** — Address non-conformities, take corrective action, and continually improve.

## What auditors look for

* Evidence that top management is actively involved.
* Documented processes that match what actually happens on the ground.
* Records of monitoring, measurement, and corrective actions.
* A culture of continual improvement — not just paperwork.

## Common non-conformities and how to avoid them

* **Missing or outdated documents** — Keep your document register current.
* **No evidence of management review** — Schedule and record regular reviews.
* **Quality objectives not measurable** — Use SMART objectives with clear KPIs.
* **Training records incomplete** — Maintain a training register for all staff.
* **Non-conformities not followed up** — Track every NCR through to closure with root cause analysis.
