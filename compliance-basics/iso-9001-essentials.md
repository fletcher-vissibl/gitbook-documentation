# ISO 9001 Essentials

### What is ISO 9001?

ISO 9001 is the international standard for Quality Management Systems (QMS). It gives you a structured way to deliver products and services that consistently meet customer and regulatory requirements. It's the most widely adopted ISO management system standard in the world.

When you select ISO 9001 as a framework in Vissibl, the platform maps your documentation, evidence, and risks to the standard's requirements clause by clause.

### The clauses you'll work with

ISO 9001 has 10 clauses. Clauses 1 to 3 cover scope and definitions. Clauses 4 to 10 are the ones that contain requirements your organisation needs to meet. These are what you'll see in the Frameworks section of Vissibl.

**Clause 4. Context of the Organisation.** Understand your business environment, who your stakeholders are, and what your QMS covers. In Vissibl, the AI Company Context questionnaire in Settings feeds directly into this.

**Clause 5. Leadership.** Top management needs to show they're committed to quality. This means having a quality policy, defined roles and responsibilities, and active involvement. Your organisational chart and documentation in Vissibl help evidence this.

**Clause 6. Planning.** Identify risks and opportunities, set quality objectives, and plan how to achieve them. Your Risk Assessment register in Vissibl is where risks live. Quality objectives can be tracked in Registers.

**Clause 7. Support.** Make sure you have the right people, skills, infrastructure, and documented information. Training records, the People directory, and your Documentation section in Vissibl all support this clause.

**Clause 8. Operation.** Plan and control your day-to-day processes, from design through to delivery. Your procedures and work instructions in Documentation cover this.

**Clause 9. Performance Evaluation.** Monitor, measure, and evaluate how your QMS is performing. This includes internal audits (My Audits), management reviews (Management Review), and the data you can pull from your Dashboard.

**Clause 10. Improvement.** Address non-conformities, take corrective action, and keep getting better. The Non Conformities board in Vissibl is where you track and close out NCRs and CAPAs.

### What auditors look for

Auditors want to see that your system isn't just documented but actually working. Specifically:

* Top management is actively involved, not just signing off.
* Your documented processes match what actually happens on the ground.
* You have records of monitoring, measurement, and corrective actions.
* There's a culture of improvement, not just paperwork for the sake of it.

Vissibl's gap analysis and Vissi Audit help you spot where your documentation and evidence fall short before the auditor does.

### Common non-conformities

* **Missing or outdated documents.** Keep your Documentation section current. Vissibl tracks versions and review dates.
* **No evidence of management review.** Schedule and record reviews in the Management Review section.
* **Quality objectives that aren't measurable.** Use specific, measurable targets. See Quality Objectives & KPIs for guidance.
* **Incomplete training records.** Maintain records in the Training section for all staff.
* **Non-conformities not followed up.** Track every NCR through to closure in Non Conformities. Auditors will check that root cause analysis was done and corrective actions were completed.
