# Preparing for Your Audit

## Stage 1 vs. Stage 2 audits

Initial certification involves two stages:

* **Stage 1 (Documentation Review)** — The auditor reviews your management system documentation to check it meets the standard's requirements. This is typically off-site or a short on-site visit.
* **Stage 2 (Implementation Audit)** — The auditor visits your site(s) to verify that your documented system is actually implemented and effective. This is the main certification audit.

## Surveillance audits explained

After certification, you'll have **surveillance audits** — typically annually. These are shorter than the initial audit and focus on:

* Continued conformity with the standard.
* Progress on any findings from previous audits.
* Specific clauses or processes selected by the auditor.

## What to expect on audit day

* The auditor will follow an **audit plan** shared in advance.
* They'll interview staff, observe processes, and review records.
* Be honest — auditors appreciate transparency over perfection.
* Have your documents, records, and evidence accessible (Vissibl makes this easy).

## Tips from real audits

* **Don't over-prepare presentations** — Auditors want to see how things really work, not a polished show.
* **Brief your team** — Make sure staff know what the audit is about and who they might speak to.
* **Have your non-conformity records ready** — Showing that you find and fix problems is a strength, not a weakness.
* **Use Vissibl's audit readiness check** — Run it before the audit to catch any gaps.
* **Keep calm** — A minor non-conformity isn't a failure. It's normal and expected.
