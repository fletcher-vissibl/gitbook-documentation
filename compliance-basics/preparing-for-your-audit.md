# Preparing for Your Audit

### Stage 1 vs. Stage 2

Initial certification involves two stages.

**Stage 1 (Documentation Review).** The auditor reviews your management system documentation to check it covers the standard's requirements. This is sometimes done off-site or as a short on-site visit. They're checking whether your system is designed to meet the standard, not whether it's fully implemented yet.

In Vissibl, your gap analysis and Vissi Audit results give you a clear picture of your documentation readiness before Stage 1.

**Stage 2 (Implementation Audit).** The auditor visits your site to verify that your documented system is actually being followed. They'll interview staff, observe processes, and review records. This is the main certification audit.

In Vissibl, your Evidence section, Non Conformities board, Risk Assessment register, and Management Review records are the evidence your auditor will want to see.

### Surveillance audits

After certification, you'll have surveillance audits, typically once a year. These are shorter than the initial audit and focus on:

* Continued conformity with the standard.
* Progress on findings from previous audits.
* Specific clauses or processes the auditor selects to check.

Vissibl's Dashboard gives you an ongoing view of your readiness between audits so you're not scrambling before each one.

### What to expect on audit day

* The auditor will share an audit plan in advance. Review it so you know which areas and people they'll focus on.
* They'll interview staff, observe how things are done, and ask to see records.
* Be honest. Auditors appreciate transparency over perfection.
* Have your documents, records, and evidence accessible. In Vissibl, everything is already in one place.

### Tips from real audits

* **Don't over-prepare presentations.** Auditors want to see how things actually work, not a polished show.
* **Brief your team.** Make sure staff know what the audit is about and who they might speak to. They don't need to be experts, just honest about how they do their work.
* **Have your NCR records ready.** Showing that you find and fix problems is a strength, not a weakness. An empty Non Conformities board is more suspicious than one with items being actively managed.
* **Run a Vissi Audit beforehand.** Go to Frameworks, open your framework, and click Run Vissi Audit in the top right. This checks your documentation and evidence against every clause and flags gaps before the auditor does.
* **Stay calm.** A minor non-conformity is normal and expected. It's not a failure.
