# Quality Objectives & KPIs

## Setting measurable quality objectives

ISO standards require you to set objectives that are measurable, monitored, and aligned with your policy. Good objectives are **SMART**:

* **Specific** — Clearly defined, not vague.
* **Measurable** — You can track progress with numbers.
* **Achievable** — Realistic given your resources.
* **Relevant** — Aligned with your quality policy and business goals.
* **Time-bound** — Has a deadline or review date.

## Examples of good KPIs

| Objective | KPI | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduce customer complaints | Number of complaints per month | < 5 per month |
| Improve on-time delivery | % of orders delivered on time | > 95% |
| Reduce workplace incidents | Lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR) | < 2.0 |
| Increase training compliance | % of staff with current training | 100% |
| Reduce waste to landfill | Tonnes of waste per quarter | 10% reduction year-on-year |

## Tracking and reporting on objectives

* Review objectives at least quarterly in your **management review**.
* Update progress in Vissibl's registers or dashboards.
* If an objective isn't being met, document why and adjust your approach.
* Auditors will ask to see evidence of monitoring — make sure it's recorded.
