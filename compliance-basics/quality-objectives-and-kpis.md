# Quality Objectives & KPIs

### Setting objectives that actually work

ISO standards require you to set objectives that are measurable, monitored, and aligned with your policy. The easiest way to think about it: if you can't measure it, it's not an objective.

Good objectives are specific (not vague), measurable (you can track progress with numbers), achievable (realistic given your resources), relevant (connected to your policy and business goals), and time-bound (has a deadline or review date).

### Examples

Here are some practical examples:

* **Reduce customer complaints.** Track complaints per month. Target: fewer than 5 per month.
* **Improve on-time delivery.** Track percentage of orders delivered on time. Target: above 95%.
* **Reduce workplace incidents.** Track lost time injury frequency rate. Target: below 2.0.
* **Increase training compliance.** Track percentage of staff with current training. Target: 100%.
* **Reduce waste to landfill.** Track tonnes of waste per quarter. Target: 10% reduction year-on-year.

### Tracking in Vissibl

Quality objectives and KPIs can be tracked in Vissibl's **Registers** section. Set up a register with columns for the objective, the KPI, the target, the current status, the responsible person, and the review date.

Review objectives at least quarterly as part of your management review (recorded in **Management Review**). If an objective isn't being met, document why and adjust your approach. Auditors will ask to see evidence of monitoring, so make sure progress is recorded.
