# Uploading Your Documents

If you have existing compliance documents (policies, procedures, manuals, certificates, evidence records), uploading them to Vissibl is one of the first things you should do.

## Where to upload

**Vault** is for existing files you want Vissibl to analyse. Upload your current documents here and Vissi will extract metadata, identify which frameworks they relate to, and use them as context when answering your questions or running a gap analysis.

**Documentation** is for documents you want to create or manage inside Vissibl. If you'd rather draft, edit, and publish policies using Vissibl's built-in editor, this is where they'll live.

Most users start by uploading everything to the Vault, then gradually move to managing key documents directly in Documentation.

## How to upload to the Vault

1. Go to **Vault** in the left sidebar.
2. Drag and drop files, or click to browse your computer.
3. Vissi will begin processing your files automatically.

## What happens after you upload

Vissi analyses your documents and maps them to your selected frameworks. Once processing is complete, you can:

* Run a gap analysis from the **Frameworks** section to see what's covered and what's missing.
* Ask **Vissi AI** questions about your documents and get answers with source citations.
* Use your uploaded documents as the foundation for generating new policies and procedures.

## Tips

* Upload as much as you have. The more context Vissi has, the more accurate your gap analysis and recommendations will be.
* Don't worry about perfect organisation. Vissi will extract metadata and categorise documents for you.
* If your compliance consultant has already uploaded documents during your onboarding session, you'll see them in the Vault when you first log in.
