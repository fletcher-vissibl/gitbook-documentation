# Uploading Documents to the Vault

The Vault is where your existing documents go. Upload everything relevant to your management system — policies, procedures, audit reports, org charts, risk registers, forms, checklists.

## To upload

1. Navigate to the **Vault** from the sidebar.
2. Drag and drop files, or click the upload button.
3. Vissibl accepts **PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, and PPTX** files.

## What happens next

* Vissibl extracts metadata (titles, dates, owners) automatically.
* Files are embedded and indexed — they become part of Vissi's AI context.
* When you ask Vissi a question, it draws on these documents to give you answers with citations.

## Tips

* **Upload everything you have**, even if it's outdated. Vissibl's gap analysis works better with more context.
* **Don't worry about perfect organisation** — AI metadata extraction handles tagging.
* You can always add more documents later. The system updates recommendations as new files arrive.
