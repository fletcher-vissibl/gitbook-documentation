# Uploading Your Documents

Your existing compliance documents are the foundation of your Vissibl account. The more context Vissi has, the more accurate your gap analysis, recommendations, and generated documentation will be.

## Before your onboarding call

Before your onboarding session, we'll ask you to upload your existing compliance documents: policies, procedures, manuals, certificates, evidence records, and anything else you're currently working with. Your compliance consultant uses these to configure your frameworks, run your initial gap analysis, and seed your account so it's ready to go when you log in for the first time.

Don't worry about perfect organisation. Upload what you have. Vissi will extract metadata and map documents to the relevant frameworks and clauses.

## After you're set up

Once your account is active, you can continue uploading documents at any time. There are two places to add them:

**Vault** is for files you want Vissibl to analyse and reference. Upload documents here and Vissi will extract metadata, identify which frameworks they relate to, and use them as context when answering your questions or running a gap analysis.

**Documentation** is for documents you want to create or manage inside Vissibl. If you'd rather draft, edit, and publish policies using Vissibl's built-in editor, this is where they'll live.

## How to upload to the Vault

1. Go to **Vault** in the left sidebar.
2. Drag and drop files, or click to browse your computer.
3. Vissi will begin processing your files automatically.

## Tips

* Upload as much as you have. Even outdated documents give Vissi useful context about your compliance history.
* If your compliance consultant has already uploaded documents during onboarding, you'll see them in the Vault when you first log in.
* As your program evolves, you can move key documents from the Vault into Documentation to manage them directly in Vissibl's editor.
