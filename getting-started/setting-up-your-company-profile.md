# Setting Up Your Company Profile

Your company profile provides Vissibl with the context it needs to tailor your compliance experience.

To set up your company profile:

1. Navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. Enter your company details — name, industry, number of employees, and locations.
3. Save your changes.

This information helps Vissi (your AI assistant) provide more relevant answers and ensures your gap analysis and risk recommendations are tailored to your organisation's size and sector.
