# Selecting Your Frameworks

Vissibl supports the following ISO frameworks:

* **ISO 9001** — Quality Management Systems
* **ISO 14001** — Environmental Management Systems
* **ISO 45001** — Occupational Health & Safety Management Systems
* **IMS** — Integrated Management System (a combination of two or more of the above)

To select your frameworks:

1. During onboarding, your Vissibl team will configure your initial framework selection.
2. You can update your selection at any time from **Settings → Company Settings**.
3. Changing your frameworks will update your gap analysis, audit checklists, and clause navigation accordingly.

If you're unsure which frameworks apply to your organisation, your onboarding team can help you decide.
