# Your First Week Checklist

Here's what we recommend tackling in your first week on Vissibl.

## Day 1–2: Get oriented

* [ ] Log in and explore the Dashboard
* [ ] Review your Gap Analysis report
* [ ] Upload any additional documents to the Vault

## Day 3–4: Set up your foundations

* [ ] Review and publish your key policies and procedures
* [ ] Set up your organisational chart (departments, roles)
* [ ] Add your core team members as users

## Day 5: Start working

* [ ] Review your risk register — check for empty fields and update owners
* [ ] Add your first vendors to the Vendor Management module
* [ ] Explore the Non-Conformities board — add any open items

## Ongoing

* [ ] Assign owners and due dates to gap findings
* [ ] Upload evidence as you collect it
* [ ] Check your Dashboard weekly for status updates

{% hint style="success" %}
Don't try to do everything at once. Vissibl is designed to grow with you — start with the fundamentals and layer in more modules over time.
{% endhint %}
