# Your First Week Checklist

Your compliance consultant has already configured your frameworks and seeded your account with documentation during onboarding. This checklist helps you review what's been set up and start making Vissibl your own.

## Day 1: Get oriented

* Log in and explore your **Dashboard**. Check your audit readiness scores across frameworks.
* Open **Frameworks** and review the clauses and gap status for each standard.
* Go to the **Vault** and check that your uploaded documents are there. Upload anything that's missing.

## Day 2: Review your documentation

* Open **Documentation** and review the policies and procedures that have been set up. Check for accuracy and completeness.
* If any documents need changes, edit them in the built-in editor and move them through the review/approval workflow.
* Ask **Vissi AI** any questions about your documents or compliance requirements.

## Day 3: Check your foundations

* Review your **Risk Assessment** register. Update owners, check scores, and fill in any gaps.
* Open the **Organisation Chart** and confirm departments, roles, and reporting lines are correct.
* Review the **People** directory and add anyone who's missing.

## Day 4: Set up your team

* Invite team members via **Settings** > **Manage Users**. Assign appropriate roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer).
* Walk your team through the Dashboard and any sections they'll use regularly.

## Day 5: Start working

* Open **Non Conformities** and add any open NCRs or CAPAs your team is currently tracking.
* Go to **Vendors** and review or add your key vendors.
* Set up your first evidence collection form in the **Evidence** section.

## Ongoing

* Assign owners and due dates to any open gap findings.
* Upload evidence as your team collects it.
* Check your Dashboard regularly for status changes and items that need attention.

Don't try to do everything at once. Vissibl is designed to grow with you. Start with what's been set up, get your team comfortable, and layer in more over time.
