# Platform Overview

Vissibl is organised around the sections you'll see in the left sidebar. Here's what each one does.

---

**Quick Actions.** A search-and-action bar you can open anytime (shortcut: K). Jump to any section, or take common actions without leaving the page: ask Vissi AI, add evidence, create an org chart, invite a vendor, create an internal audit, or add a risk assessment.

**Dashboard.** Your starting point. See audit readiness across your frameworks, open risks, pending approvals, and what needs attention, all in one view.

**Vissi AI.** Your AI co-pilot. Ask compliance questions in plain English and get answers grounded in your documents and company context, with source citations. Vissi can also take action: create and update documents, draft policies, and carry out tasks across the platform on your behalf.

### Compliance

**Frameworks.** Where you manage the ISO standards your organisation operates under. Each framework shows your current readiness score, the clauses you need to address, and any gaps Vissi has identified. You can also run a Vissi Audit from here to check your status.

**Documentation.** Where you draft, review, approve, and publish policies, procedures, and manuals using Vissibl's built-in editor. Documents are organised by framework.

**Vault.** Your secure document store. Upload your existing files here. Vissi will analyse them, extract metadata, and use them as context when answering your questions or identifying gaps.

**Registers.** Flexible tables for tracking interested parties, assets, legal requirements, and anything else your program requires. Add custom columns, assign owners, and attach files.

**Evidence.** Where you create forms and collection mechanisms to capture proof that your procedures are being followed. This is how your team records the day-to-day evidence that auditors need to see.

### Risk

**Risk Assessment.** Track and score risks, assign owners, and monitor how treatments are progressing. Each risk links back to the frameworks and controls it affects.

### Assurance

**My Audits.** Plan and run internal and external audits by department. Select the clauses you want to cover and Vissibl generates the checklists for you.

**Non Conformities.** Track NCRs and CAPAs on a visual board. Drag items between statuses, document root causes, and link corrective actions to the relevant procedures.

**Management Review.** Record your management review meetings, including attendees, agenda items, review inputs, and decisions. Filter by framework to keep reviews organised.

### Vendor Management

**Vendors.** Onboard vendors, run automated research on their compliance status, score risk, and track certifications and expiry dates in one place.

**Vendor Risks.** View and manage risks specific to your vendor relationships, separate from your organisational risk register.

**Vendor Assessment Setup.** Configure the criteria and scoring you use to evaluate vendors during onboarding and ongoing reviews.

### People & Operations

**People.** A directory of the people in your organisation. Record roles, departments, and contact details. This is a reference list, not platform access.

**Organisation Chart.** Define your departments, roles, and reporting lines. This feeds into audit planning and document assignments across the platform.

**Training.** Assign and track compliance training courses for your team. Each course shows estimated duration, module count, and completion status.
