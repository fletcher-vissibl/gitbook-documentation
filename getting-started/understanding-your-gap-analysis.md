# Understanding Your Gap Analysis Report

After your documents are uploaded, Vissibl runs a gap analysis against your selected ISO framework(s). This shows you what's covered and what's missing.

The report is organised into **thematic buckets** — plain-English groupings like "Document Control," "Risk Management," or "Customer Feedback" rather than raw clause numbers.

## Statuses

Each bucket shows one of three statuses:

* **Present** — Documentation exists and addresses the requirement.
* **Missing** — No documentation was found for this requirement.
* **Needs Review** — Something exists but may be incomplete or outdated.

Items are also labelled as **Required** (must-have for compliance) or **Recommended** (best practice but not strictly mandatory).

## What to do with it

* **Focus on Required + Missing items first** — these are your audit blockers.
* For each gap, you can assign an owner and due date to track remediation.
* If a gap is flagged but you know the document exists elsewhere, simply upload it to the Vault and the analysis will update.

{% hint style="info" %}
The gap analysis is based on what's been uploaded. If your quality manual is web-based or lives in another system, the analysis won't see it until you upload a copy. This doesn't mean there's an actual gap — just a data gap.
{% endhint %}
