# Inviting Users & Understanding Roles

To get your team onto Vissibl:

1. Go to **Settings → User Management** (or the Users section in your sidebar).
2. Click **Add User**.
3. Enter their email, select their role, and set a temporary password.

## Roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full access to everything, including billing and account settings. Typically the QA/QHSE manager or account holder. |
| **Admin** | Full access to all modules and settings, except billing. |
| **Editor** | Can create, edit, and submit documents and records. Cannot publish or manage users. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to published documents and dashboards. |

## Tips

* Start by adding the people who need to approve documents or own risks.
* You can change roles at any time.
* For compliance training and policy sign-offs (coming soon), users will need individual accounts to track their acknowledgements.
