# Your First Login — What You'll See

When you first log in, you'll land on the **Dashboard**. Depending on how far along your onboarding is, you may already see:

* Your audit readiness status for the selected framework(s)
* Any risk flags from vendor research or your risk register
* Non-conformity summaries
* A compliance progress overview

From the left sidebar, you can navigate to every module: Documents, Vault, Risks, Audits, Registers, Vendors, and more.

Don't worry if things look sparse on day one — the Dashboard populates as you add documents, risks, and evidence.
