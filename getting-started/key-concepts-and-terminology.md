# Key Concepts & Terminology

Terms you'll see across the platform that aren't covered in the [Platform Overview](/platform-overview).

---

**Audit Readiness.** A status score shown against each framework (e.g. 85%, "Good" or 0%, "Critical"). It reflects whether your documentation and evidence are sufficient to pass an audit based on what Vissibl can see. You'll find this on the Dashboard and inside each framework.

**Gap Analysis.** An AI-driven check of your documents against your selected frameworks. It shows what's in place, what's missing, and what needs review. You can trigger a gap analysis from the Frameworks section by clicking "Run Vissi Audit."

**Clause.** A specific requirement within a framework. For example, ISO 9001 has clauses 4 through 10, each covering a different area like leadership, planning, or operations. Vissibl maps your documents and evidence to clauses so you can see coverage at a glance.

**Framework.** A compliance standard your organisation follows, such as ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 14001 (Environmental), or ISO 45001 (Health & Safety). You select your frameworks during onboarding and manage them in the Frameworks section.

**IMS (Integrated Management System).** When you manage multiple frameworks together, sharing common controls and documentation rather than running each one separately. Vissibl supports this by mapping shared clauses across frameworks.

**Non-Conformity (NC).** When something doesn't meet a requirement. This could come from an audit finding, a customer complaint, or something your team spots internally.

**CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action).** The structured response to a non-conformity. A corrective action fixes what went wrong. A preventive action stops it from happening again. Both are tracked in the Non Conformities section.

**Evidence.** Proof that your procedures are being followed. This could be a completed form, a photo, a signed record, or any documentation that shows work was done to standard. Auditors rely on evidence to verify compliance.
