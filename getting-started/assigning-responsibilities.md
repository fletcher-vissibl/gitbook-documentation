# Assigning Responsibilities Across Modules

Once your team is set up, assign ownership across Vissibl's modules to ensure accountability.

## Where to assign responsibilities

* **Documents** — Assign document owners and approvers during the review workflow.
* **Risk Register** — Assign a risk owner to each identified risk.
* **Non-Conformities** — Assign an owner responsible for investigating and closing each NCR.
* **Audits** — Assign audit leads and auditees by department.
* **Registers** — Assign owners to training records, assets, and other register entries.
* **Vendor Management** — Assign a relationship owner for each vendor.

## Best practices

* Align responsibilities with your organisational chart — use department heads as default owners.
* Don't overload a single person. Spread ownership across your team.
* Review assignments periodically, especially after organisational changes.
