# Accepting Your Invite & Setting Your Password

When your account is created, you'll receive an email with a secure setup link.

1. **Open the email** from Vissibl and click the setup link.
2. **Create your password.** The link is time-bound for security — if it expires, contact your admin or our support team for a new one.
3. Once your password is set, you'll be taken to your Vissibl workspace.

If you're the first user on your account, your Vissibl onboarding team will activate your account after your initial setup session. You'll typically receive the activation email immediately after that call.
