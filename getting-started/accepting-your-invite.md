# Accepting Your Invite & Setting Your Password

## Account owners

After your onboarding call with our compliance team, we'll configure your account and send you an activation email.

1. Open the email from Vissibl and click the setup link.
2. Create your password.
3. You'll be taken straight to your workspace.

The setup link is time-bound for security. If it expires, contact our support team and we'll send a new one.

## Team members

If someone on your team has invited you to their Vissibl workspace, you'll receive an email invitation.

1. Open the email and click the invite link.
2. Create your password.
3. You'll land in your team's workspace with the permissions your admin has assigned.

If you're not sure who invited you or you didn't receive the email, check your spam folder or ask your account admin to resend the invite.

## Inviting team members

If you're an account owner or admin and want to add people to your workspace:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Manage Users** (under Security & Access).
2. Click **+ User** in the top right.
3. Enter their details and assign a role:
   - **Owner** can manage the account, users, and all settings.
   - **Admin** has full access to all modules and settings, except billing. Can manage users.
   - **Editor** can create and modify content across the platform.
   - **Viewer** can view content but not make changes.
4. They'll receive an email invitation to set up their password and access the workspace.
