# Company Profile

Go to **Settings** > **Company Profile** to manage your organisation's details. This information is used across the platform and helps Vissi provide more relevant recommendations.

You can update:

* **Logo Image.** Upload your company logo (PNG, JPG, or GIF, under 10 MB).
* **Company Name.**
* **Tax ID.**
* **Business Licence.**
* **Website.**
* **Primary Business Location.** Select your country from the dropdown.
* **Industry.** Select your industry from the dropdown.

## Frameworks

Your selected ISO frameworks are displayed at the bottom of the Company Profile page. These are the frameworks you chose during onboarding (e.g. ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001). If you need to change your framework selection, contact your Vissibl account manager.

## Workspace Deletion

At the bottom of the page, the **Delete Company** button permanently removes your workspace and revokes access for all team members. This action cannot be undone.
