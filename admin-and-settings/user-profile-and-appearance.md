# User Profile & Appearance

## User Profile

Go to **Settings** > **User Profile** to manage your personal details.

You can update:

* **Profile Image.** Upload a photo (PNG, JPG, or GIF, under 10 MB).
* **First Name** and **Last Name.**
* **Email ID.**
* **Phone.**
* **Job Role.** Select your role from the dropdown (e.g. Quality Manager).

Click **Change Password** in the top right to update your password.

## Appearance

Go to **Settings** > **Appearance** to adjust visual preferences for your workspace.
