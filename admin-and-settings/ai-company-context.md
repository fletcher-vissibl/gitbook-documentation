# AI Company Context

Go to **Settings** > **AI Company Context** to provide Vissi with detailed information about your organisation. The more context you provide here, the more accurate Vissi's gap analysis, document generation, and recommendations will be.

The questionnaire is organised into tabs:

* **Company Overview.** Your industry, products and services, operational processes, environmental factors, outsourced activities, business processes, and site locations.
* **Context of the Organization.** Additional context about your organisational environment.
* **Communication with Interested Parties.** How you communicate with stakeholders.
* **Policies.** Your existing policy landscape.
* **Responsibilities & Authorities.** Roles and decision-making structures.
* **Identifying Key Risks.** (And potentially additional tabs depending on your frameworks.)

Answer the questions in each tab as thoroughly as you can. This information feeds directly into how Vissi generates documents, identifies gaps, and provides recommendations. You can return and update your answers at any time.
