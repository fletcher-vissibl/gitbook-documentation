# Managing Users

Go to **Settings** > **Manage Users** to control who has access to your Vissibl workspace and what they can do.

## The user list

You'll see a table of all users on your workspace showing their name, email, phone number, and role.

## Adding a user

Click **+ User** in the top right to invite someone new. Enter their details and assign a role. They'll receive an email invitation to set up their password and access the workspace.

## Roles

There are four roles:

* **Owner.** Full access to everything, including billing and account settings. Can manage users and delete the workspace.
* **Admin.** Full access to all modules and settings, except billing. Can manage users.
* **Editor.** Can create, edit, and submit documents and records. Cannot publish documents or manage users.
* **Viewer.** Read-only access to published documents and dashboards.

## Removing a user

Select a user from the list to edit their details or remove them. Removing a user revokes their access immediately.
