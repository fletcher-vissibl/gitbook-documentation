# Settings

Settings is where you manage your account, your company details, your team, and your security preferences. Open Settings by clicking the **gear icon** in the top right of the screen.

Settings is organised into four sections:

**Profile & Identity.** Your personal profile and appearance preferences.

**Company.** Your organisation's details, the context Vissi uses to understand your business, and your uploaded documents.

**Security & Access.** Two-factor authentication and user management.

**Account.** Sign out of your workspace.
