# Company Settings

Update your organisation's details and framework selections.

## Updating company information

Navigate to **Settings → Company Settings** to update:

* Company name and contact details
* Industry and sector
* Number of employees
* Locations and sites

This information helps Vissi provide more relevant recommendations.

## Changing your selected frameworks

You can add or remove ISO frameworks at any time:

1. Go to **Settings → Company Settings**.
2. Update your framework selection (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, or IMS).
3. Save your changes.

Changing frameworks will update your gap analysis, audit checklists, and Frameworks Hub navigation.
