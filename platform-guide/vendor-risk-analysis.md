# Vendor Risk Analysis

Vendor risk analysis combines AI-powered research with structured risk scoring to give you a clear picture of each vendor's risk profile. You can access it from the **Risks** tab on any vendor profile, or through the **Vendor Risks** section in the sidebar.

---

### The Risks tab (on a vendor profile)

Click the **Risks** tab on any vendor to see their full risk analysis. At the top you'll see:

* **Overall risk rating** and score (e.g. Medium / Conditional Approval, 38/100).
* A **Recommendation** from Vissi summarising the vendor's risk profile and what to watch for.
* Key metrics: Risk Score, Total Risks Identified, Open Risks, and Verified Certifications.

### Risk visualisations

Below the summary, three charts break down the vendor's risks:

* **Risks by severity.** How many risks fall into each severity level (Major, Moderate, Minor, Catastrophic, Insignificant).
* **Risk score by category.** A horizontal bar chart showing risk scores across categories: Operational, Compliance, Financial, Legal/Compliance, Information Security, and Data Integrity.
* **Risk level distribution.** The proportion of High vs Medium vs Low risks.

### Vendor Risk Heatmap

A heatmap plotting each risk by likelihood (Rare through Almost Certain) and severity (Insignificant through Catastrophic). Each cell is colour-coded by risk level, and individual risks are shown by their ID so you can identify which risks sit where.

### Risk Register

Below the heatmap, a table lists every identified risk with its ID, vendor, description, category, likelihood, impact, risk score, and risk rating. Click **See more** on any row to expand the full description.

### Certifications

At the bottom of the Risks tab, you'll see the vendor's certifications and their verification status (e.g. "In Progress", "Claimed"). These are identified during the Vissi Research process.

### Re-running the risk analysis

Click **Re-run Vendor Risk** in the top right to regenerate the risk analysis with the latest data.

### Downloading the due diligence report

From the **Vissi Research** tab on any vendor profile, click the download icon to export a branded PDF. The report includes an at-a-glance summary (data points checked, overall rating, risk counts), an executive risk summary, a risk category breakdown with ratings and rationale for each category, a company overview, key findings across all areas (licensing, key people, legal, safety, sanctions, reputation), and a clear recommendation with next steps. The report cites 24+ sources and is formatted for sharing with stakeholders who need to approve or review vendor relationships.

---

### Vendor Risks (sidebar section)

The **Vendor Risks** section in the sidebar gives you a consolidated view of risks across all your vendors.

Risks are organised into category tabs matching your vendor categories (e.g. Software Vendors Risks, Hardware Vendors Risks). Each row shows the Vendor Risk ID, vendor name, description, category, likelihood, impact, risk score, risk rating, and whether the risk has been accepted.

### Adding a vendor risk manually

Click **+ Vendor Risk** in the top right. Fill in:

* **Vendor Risk ID.** A reference for the risk.
* **Description.** What the risk is.
* **Category.** The type of risk (Operational, Compliance, Financial, Information Security, Legal/Compliance, Data Integrity).
* **Likelihood** and **Impact.** Used to calculate the risk score.
* **Risk Accepted.** Whether your organisation accepts this risk.
* **Risk Treatment Strategy.** How you plan to respond (e.g. Mitigate/Reduce, Accept, Avoid).
* **Treatment Action Plan.** The specific actions you'll take.

Click any row to open the full detail panel with all fields plus an Attachments tab.

### Tips

* Most vendor risks are generated automatically by Vissi when you run a risk analysis. You can also add risks manually for things Vissi wouldn't pick up on, like relationship or contractual risks.
* Use the heatmap to prioritise. Risks in the top-right corner (high likelihood, high severity) need attention first.
* The Vendor Risks sidebar view is useful for comparing risk profiles across vendors side by side.
