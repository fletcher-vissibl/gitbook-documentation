# Vendor Management

Onboard, evaluate, and monitor your suppliers with Vissibl's Vendor Management module.

## Adding a vendor

1. Navigate to **Vendor Management** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Vendor**.
3. Enter the vendor's details — name, contact information, and services provided.

## Vendor research (automatic OSINT report)

When you add a vendor, Vissibl can automatically run an OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) research report. This pulls publicly available information about the vendor to help you assess risk before onboarding.

## Understanding vendor research reports & data sources

Research reports may include:

* Company registration and financial data
* News and media mentions
* Certification status (ISO, industry-specific)
* Sanctions and watchlist checks
* Online reputation indicators

## The onboarding form

Send a standardised onboarding form to new vendors to collect:

* Company details and certifications
* Insurance information
* Compliance documentation
* Key contacts

## Vendor evaluation & scoring

Score vendors against criteria that matter to your organisation:

* Quality performance
* Delivery reliability
* Compliance status
* Communication and responsiveness

## Continuous evaluation

Vendor management isn't a one-time activity. Vissibl supports ongoing monitoring:

* Schedule regular re-evaluations.
* Track certification expiry dates.
* Monitor for changes in vendor risk profile.

## Setting research frequency

Configure how often Vissibl refreshes vendor research data. Options typically range from monthly to annually.

## Vendor risk flags on your Dashboard

High-risk vendors and those with expiring certifications surface on your Dashboard as alerts, so nothing falls through the cracks.
