# The Vault

The Vault is your secure document store — the place to upload and manage all your existing files.

## What is the Vault?

Think of the Vault as your digital filing cabinet. It stores your existing documents and makes them searchable, while feeding them into Vissi's AI context so you get smarter answers and better gap analysis.

## Uploading files

* **Drag and drop** files directly into the Vault, or click the upload button.
* **Supported formats:** PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, PPTX.
* There's no limit to how many files you can upload — the more context, the better.

## How the Vault feeds AI context

Every file you upload is:

1. **Parsed** — Vissibl extracts the text content.
2. **Indexed** — The content becomes searchable.
3. **Embedded** — The content feeds into Vissi's AI, so when you ask questions, Vissi can reference your actual documents with citations.

## Finding documents in the Vault

Use the search bar at the top of the Vault to find documents by name, content, or metadata. Vissibl's AI-powered metadata extraction tags files automatically.

## Vault vs. Documents — what goes where

| | Vault | Documents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What goes here** | Existing files you upload (PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets) | Policies and procedures you create or edit in Vissibl's editor |
| **Editing** | View only — files are stored as-is | Full editing with the Notion-style editor |
| **Lifecycle** | No workflow — files are stored for reference and AI context | Draft → Review → Approve → Publish |
| **Best for** | Audit reports, legacy documents, evidence, external files | Active policies, procedures, manuals |
