# Training

Track your team's training records, certifications, and compliance requirements.

## Adding training records

1. Navigate to the **Training Register** from the Registers section.
2. Add a new entry for each training activity.
3. Include the trainee, training topic, date completed, and next due date.

## Tracking training status & due dates

The training register shows you at a glance:

* Who has completed their required training.
* What training is overdue.
* Upcoming training due dates.

## Compliance training vs. general skills training

* **Compliance training** — Required by your ISO framework(s) or regulations. Examples: health and safety induction, environmental awareness, quality procedures.
* **General skills training** — Professional development and role-specific skills. Not strictly required for compliance, but good practice to track.
