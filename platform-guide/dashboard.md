# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your compliance command center — the first thing you see when you log in.

## What the Dashboard shows

At a glance, the Dashboard gives you:

* **Audit readiness status** — A summary of how prepared you are for your next audit, broken down by framework.
* **Risk summary** — Your top risks and their current status, pulled from the Risk Register.
* **Non-conformity tracker** — Open NCRs and CAPAs, with their current stage.
* **Compliance progress** — An overview of your documentation and evidence coverage.

## Refreshing your compliance status

Your Dashboard updates automatically as you:

* Upload documents to the Vault
* Publish policies and procedures
* Close non-conformities
* Update your risk register
* Complete audit findings

You can also manually refresh individual widgets to see the latest data.

## Tips

* Check your Dashboard at least weekly to stay on top of what needs attention.
* Use the risk and non-conformity summaries to prioritise your team's work.
* The audit readiness indicator is your best friend in the lead-up to an audit.
