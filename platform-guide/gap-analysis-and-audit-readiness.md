# Gap Analysis & Audit Readiness

Vissibl's gap analysis compares your uploaded documents against your selected ISO framework(s) and tells you exactly where you stand.

## Understanding your gap recommendations

After uploading documents to the Vault, Vissibl analyses them against the requirements of your selected frameworks. Results are grouped into plain-English thematic buckets rather than raw clause numbers.

## Statuses

Each requirement shows one of three statuses:

* **Present** — Documentation exists and addresses the requirement.
* **Missing** — No documentation was found for this requirement.
* **Needs Review** — Something exists but may be incomplete or outdated.

## Required vs. Recommended

* **Required** — Must-have for compliance. These are your audit blockers.
* **Recommended** — Best practice items. Not strictly mandatory, but they strengthen your system.

## Running an audit readiness check

Your audit readiness status updates automatically as you upload documents, publish policies, and close gaps. Check the Dashboard for your current readiness score.

## Acting on gap findings

For each gap identified:

1. **Assign an owner** — Who is responsible for addressing this gap?
2. **Set a due date** — When should it be resolved?
3. **Take action** — Create the missing document, update an existing one, or upload evidence.
4. **Track progress** — The gap status updates automatically as you resolve items.

{% hint style="info" %}
If a gap is flagged but you know the document exists elsewhere, upload it to the Vault. The analysis will update once the new document is processed.
{% endhint %}
