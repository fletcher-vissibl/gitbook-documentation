# My Tasks

## My Tasks

My Tasks is your personal compliance inbox. It collects every item across the platform that requires your attention — documents waiting for your review, controls assigned to you, risks you own, training you need to complete, and more — all in one place.

### How it works

When you open My Tasks, Vissibl fetches every pending item linked to your user account across all compliance areas. Items are grouped by type so you can work through them systematically or filter to the area you want to focus on.

### Task types

Use the tab bar at the top to filter by type. Each tab shows a count of open items.

| Tab                  | What it contains                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **All**              | Every pending item across all categories                                            |
| **Controls**         | Security or compliance controls assigned to you, with their current workflow status |
| **Documents**        | Policy and procedure documents awaiting your review, approval, or acknowledgment    |
| **Registers**        | Rows in compliance registers (e.g. asset register, legal register) assigned to you  |
| **Evidence**         | Evidence collection items assigned to you                                           |
| **Risks**            | Risk register entries you own or are assigned to                                    |
| **Non-Conformities** | Audit findings and corrective actions requiring your input                          |
| **Vendor Approvals** | Vendor onboarding requests at a stage that requires your decision                   |
| **Training**         | Security awareness or compliance courses assigned to you                            |

### How items are displayed

**Cards** (Documents, Controls, Training, Vendor Approvals)

Each item is shown as a card with the name, a brief description or excerpt, and a status badge. Click anywhere on the card, or use the action link at the bottom, to navigate directly to the item.

* Document cards show the version number and one or more pending action badges: **Pending Review**, **Pending Approval**, or **Pending Acknowledgment**.
* Control cards show the control code and name, a description excerpt, and the workflow status: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, or **Implemented**.
* Training cards show the course name, description, due date, and schedule type (e.g. Annual, One-Time).
* Vendor Approval cards show the vendor name, contact email, and the current approval stage: **Onboarding Review**, **Document Review**, or **Final Approval**.

**Tables** (Registers, Evidence, Risks, Non-Conformities)

These items are displayed in a table that mirrors the columns configured for that specific register or evidence table. Items are grouped by their source table, so if you have tasks from two different risk registers they appear in two separate tables. Click **View** on any row to jump directly to that item.

### Navigating to an item

Every task links directly to the relevant section of the platform so you can take action without searching for it manually. You are taken to the exact record, row, or workflow that needs your attention.
