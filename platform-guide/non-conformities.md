# Non Conformities

Non Conformities is where you track NCRs and CAPAs. It uses a visual board layout so you can see the status of every item at a glance and move them through the resolution process.

---

### The board view

When you open Non Conformities, you'll see a kanban-style board with four columns: **Opened**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, and **Closed**. Each card shows a description of the issue, its NCCA ID, and priority level.

Drag and drop cards between columns to update their status as work progresses.

You can also switch to **List View** using the toggle at the top of the page.

### Filtering by audit

Use the dropdown in the top left to filter non-conformities by audit (e.g. AUD-MAY-2026, AUD-EXT-13). Select **General** to see items not linked to a specific audit, or select multiple audits to see them together. This lets you track which findings came from which audit.

### Creating a non-conformity

Click **+ Non Conformity** in the top right, or click **+ Add Non Conformity** at the bottom of any column to add an item directly in that status.

### Detail view

Click any card to open the detail panel on the right. This is where you document and manage the full lifecycle of the issue:

* **NCCA ID.** Automatically assigned.
* **Type.** Major or Minor.
* **Observation.** A description of what was found.
* **Source.** Where the issue came from (e.g. Internal Audit, External Audit, Customer Complaint).
* **Date Reported.** When the issue was identified.
* **Priority.** High, Medium, or Low.
* **Responsibility (Owner).** The person responsible for resolving it.
* **Clauses.** Link the non-conformity to specific framework clauses it relates to.
* **Root Cause.** Document why the issue occurred.
* **Corrective Action.** What will be done to fix it.
* **Target Closure Date.** When the corrective action should be completed.
* **Actual Closure Date.** When it was actually closed.
* **Evidence.** Upload files to prove the corrective action has been implemented.

The panel also has an **Attachments** tab for any additional supporting documents.

### Tips

* Use the board to run your regular NCR review meetings. The visual layout makes it easy to walk through open items as a team.
* Filter by audit before a review meeting to focus on findings from a specific audit cycle.
* Non-conformity counts and statuses feed into your Dashboard, so keeping the board current keeps your overview accurate.
