# Documentation

Documentation is where you create, edit, and manage your compliance documents: manuals, policies, procedures, and forms. Every document goes through a managed lifecycle and is organised by framework.

***

### Navigating Documentation

Documents are organised by framework. Use the dropdown at the top of the page to switch between frameworks (e.g. ISO 9001, ISO 14001), or expand Documentation in the sidebar to jump to a specific one.

Within each framework, documents are grouped into four categories: **Manuals**, **Policies**, **Procedures**, and **Forms**. Each document shows its name, version number, author, last updated date, and current status.

### Document statuses

Every document has a status that tracks where it is in the lifecycle:

* **Draft.** The document is being written or edited. Only visible to authors and editors.
* **Under Review.** The document has been submitted for review and is awaiting feedback.
* **Rejected.** The reviewer has sent the document back for changes.
* **Published.** The document has been approved and is the current active version.

### Creating a document

1. Navigate to the framework and category where you want to add the document.
2. Click **+ Add Document** to create a new document in the editor, or click **Upload** to bring in an existing file.
3. The editor works like Notion. Type directly, or press **/** on a new line to open the block menu. From there you can search and insert blocks like text, headings, tables, lists, and more.
4. You can also paste content directly from Word and the formatting will carry over.

### Using Vissi in the editor

Click the **Vissi sparkle icon** (next to the Publish button) to get AI assistance with your document. Vissi can generate a draft based on your company context, or reformat and restructure an existing document.

### Document details panel

Click the **chevron icon** (>>) at the top of any document to open the details panel on the right. This shows metadata including version number, document type, status, visibility, authors, creation date, last modified date, department, and any notes.

The panel also has a **Workflow history** tab where you can see the review and approval history for that document.

### Sending a document for review

When your document is ready, click the **Publish** button dropdown and select the option to send it for review. You'll be asked to:

* Confirm the document name and version.
* Assign a **Reviewer** and **Approver**. These must be users already added to your workspace in **Settings** > **Manage Users**.
* Add a version description.
* Set the document type and department.
* Choose an effective date (immediately upon approval, or a specific date).
* Optionally enable **Reader Acknowledgment Workflow**, which collects read confirmations from relevant team members based on the document type.

Click **Submit** to send the document into the review workflow.

### Exporting documents

Documents can be exported as PDF or DOCX from the three-dot menu (**...**) on any document.
