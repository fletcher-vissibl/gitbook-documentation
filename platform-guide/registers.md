# Registers

Registers are flexible, structured tables for tracking any type of record in your management system.

## What are registers?

Think of registers as customisable spreadsheets built into Vissibl. They're designed for tracking things like training records, assets, interested parties, and anything else your management system requires.

## Default registers

Vissibl comes with several pre-configured registers:

* **Training Register** — Track employee training records, certifications, and due dates.
* **Management System Improvement (MSI)** — Log improvement opportunities.
* **Interested Parties** — Record your stakeholders and their requirements.
* **Legal & Regulatory Requirements** — Track applicable laws and regulations.

## Creating a custom register

1. Navigate to **Registers** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Register**.
3. Give it a name and start adding columns.

## Adding columns

Registers support multiple column types:

* **Text** — Free-form text fields.
* **People** — Assign users from your team.
* **Dates** — Due dates, completion dates, review dates.
* **Files** — Attach documents as evidence.
* **Checkboxes** — Simple yes/no tracking.
* **Dropdowns** — Predefined options for status or category.

## Renaming and customising registers

Click on a register name to rename it. Reorder columns by dragging them, or hide columns you don't need.

## Importing data (CSV)

If you have existing records in spreadsheets, you can import them via CSV:

1. Open the register you want to populate.
2. Click **Import** and select your CSV file.
3. Map your CSV columns to the register's columns.
4. Review and confirm the import.
