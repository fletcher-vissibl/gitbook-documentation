# Audits

Plan and run internal and external audits directly in Vissibl, with auto-generated checklists tied to your selected frameworks.

## Internal vs. external audits

* **Internal audits** — Conducted by your own team to assess compliance before an external audit. Essential for identifying and fixing issues proactively.
* **External audits** — Conducted by a certification body. Vissibl helps you prepare and track findings.

## Creating an audit

1. Navigate to **Audits** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Audit**.
3. Select the audit type (internal or external).
4. Choose the department(s) being audited.
5. Select the relevant clauses from your framework(s).

## Department-based auditing approach

Vissibl structures audits by department, making it easy to:

* Assign audit leads per department.
* Focus on the clauses most relevant to each area of the business.
* Track findings at the departmental level.

## Selecting relevant clauses

When creating an audit, select which framework clauses to include. Vissibl will auto-generate a checklist based on your selection.

## Audit checklists

For each selected clause, the checklist provides:

* **Conform** — Evidence exists and the requirement is met.
* **Non-Conform** — A gap or deviation was found (this creates a linked NCR).

## Adding evidence to audit findings

Attach files, link documents, or add notes as evidence for each checklist item. This creates a clear audit trail.

## Audit scheduling

Schedule recurring internal audits to maintain your audit programme. Set frequency, assign responsibilities, and get reminders as audit dates approach.
