# Vendor Assessment Setup

Vendor Assessment Setup is where you configure the forms and criteria used to onboard and evaluate vendors. Set these up before you start inviting vendors so the process runs smoothly from the first invite.

There are three tools, each shown as a card on the main page.

---

### Onboard Form

The onboarding questionnaire that vendors must complete when they're added to the system. This collects essential information about their business, certifications, policies, and capabilities.

**What it collects:** Business and company details, compliance certifications and licences, supporting documents, and experience and capabilities.

**Building the form.** Click **Onboard Form** to open the form builder. Questions are organised into groups (shown as tabs at the top, e.g. Financials, Group 3). You can:

* Add, edit, and reorder questions using the drag handles.
* Create new groups with the **+** button.
* Use the **Vissi sparkle icon** to help generate questions.
* Click **Preview** to see exactly what the vendor will see when they receive the form.

The form is sent to vendors when you invite them. Their completed responses are visible on the **Onboard Form** tab of their vendor profile.

---

### Vendor Evaluation

Evaluation questionnaires your team uses internally to assess vendors after they've completed the onboarding form. These help you review vendor responses and assess their suitability based on predefined standards.

**Examples of evaluation criteria:** Quality and compliance standards, industry certifications, relevant experience, and completeness of submitted documentation.

**Managing questionnaires.** Click **Vendor Evaluation** to see your existing questionnaires, or click **+ Evaluation Questionnaire** to create a new one. Questions are organised into groups (e.g. People, Financial Questions) and each question can be a short answer, multiple choice, or other question type.

Completed evaluations are visible on the **Eval Form** tab of the vendor's profile.

---

### Vendor Rating Form

An internal scoring form your team uses to rate vendor performance. This gives you a structured, consistent way to score vendors across specific requirements.

**Building a rating form.** Click **Vendor Rating Form** to open the builder. You can:

* Add sections with **+ Add Section** (e.g. Quality, Delivery, Communication).
* Define specific requirements within each section.
* Use the **star-based scoring** system to rate each requirement.
* Toggle **Feedback** on or off for each requirement to allow written comments.
* Add a description for each requirement to guide evaluators.

---

### Tips

* Set up all three forms before inviting your first vendor. This ensures you have a consistent process from the start.
* Use the Preview function on the Onboard Form to check the vendor experience before sending invitations.
* You can create multiple Vendor Evaluation questionnaires for different vendor types or assessment purposes.
