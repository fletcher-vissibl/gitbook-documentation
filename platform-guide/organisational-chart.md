# Organisational Chart

Define your company's structure, departments, and reporting lines — and connect them to your compliance activities.

## Setting up your org chart

1. Navigate to **Organisational Chart** from the sidebar.
2. Add your top-level departments and business units.
3. Add roles and assign users to them.

## Adding departments & units

Create departments that reflect your actual organisational structure. Common examples:

* Quality
* Health & Safety
* Environmental
* Operations
* Human Resources
* Finance

## Assigning users to roles

Link Vissibl users to roles within your org chart. This helps with:

* Assigning document ownership.
* Routing approval workflows.
* Planning department-based audits.

## How the org chart connects to audits

Your organisational chart feeds directly into the Audits module:

* When you create an audit, select departments from your org chart.
* Audit leads and auditees are drawn from the roles you've defined.
* This ensures audit coverage across your entire organisation.
